# Measured experiment results

Evaluated 1024 held-out examples. One paired training seed; this is an educational comparison, not a statistical ranking.

| Schedule | Updates | Parameters | Training seconds | Noise MSE at alpha_bar ≈ 0.5 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| linear | 8000 | 1140353 | 298.7 | 0.041126 ± 0.000643 SE |
| cosine | 8000 | 1140353 | 297.2 | 0.040569 ± 0.000633 SE |

All paired controls verified. Full per-timestep measurements, signal-matched comparisons, dataset checksums, initial-weight hashes, checkpoint hashes and environment information are in [metrics.json](metrics.json).

Actual and predicted noise share a fixed signed color scale of −3 to +3 standard deviations. Clean-image display clips to [−1, 1]; both unclipped and clipped reconstruction MSE are recorded.

Sample grids and GIFs are generated by the trained EMA models. Test labels are used only for viewer captions; training is unconditional.
